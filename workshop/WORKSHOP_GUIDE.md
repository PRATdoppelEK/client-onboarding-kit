## Section 1: Welcome (15 min)

### Facilitator script:
*"Before we start, I want to know one thing from each of you: what is the most repetitive, annoying task in your job that you wish someone else would do?"*

Go around the room. Write answers on whiteboard.
**This becomes your demo agenda** — show how AI handles their specific pain points.

### Key message:
*"By the end of today, you'll know whether AI can help with these exact tasks — and roughly what it would cost."*

---

## Section 2: What is AI? (20 min)

### Slide 1: The honest definition
*"AI is software that learns patterns from data and uses those patterns to make predictions or decisions."*

That's it. No magic. No robots taking over. Just pattern recognition at scale.

### Slide 2: The three things AI does well
1. **Reads and finds** — faster than any human
2. **Spots patterns** — across more data than any human can see
3. **Predicts problems** — before they become expensive

### Slide 3: What AI cannot do (equally important)
- Make final business decisions (that's your job)
- Work without data (garbage in = garbage out)
- Guarantee 100% accuracy (nothing does)
- Understand context you haven't given it

### Discussion question (5 min):
*"Which of the three things AI does well would be most valuable for your business right now?"*

## Section 3: Live Demo (20 min)

### For inverter/energy clients — show inverter-predictive-ai:
1. Open the fleet dashboard: *"Here are 20 inverter units. Notice units 1–5 are red."*
2. Click on a critical unit: *"This unit has 23% health. The IGBT junction temperature has been rising for weeks. RUL: 12 days."*
3. Move the sliders: *"What if the fan vibration increases? Watch the health score drop in real time."*
4. Show the alert: *"This is what your field engineer receives — exactly what to fix and why."*

**Key point:** *"No data science degree needed to use this dashboard."*

### For franchise/CA clients — show franchise-accounting-ai:
1. Open anomaly results: *"15 outlets, 47 anomalies detected automatically."*
2. Click a HIGH priority alert: *"This outlet submitted round numbers 90% of the time — statistically impossible for real sales."*
3. Show royalty gap: *"₹18 lakh in unpaid royalties identified automatically — in data your accountant was already looking at."*

### For tender/BESS clients — show tender-compliance-rag:
1. Ask: *"Is Grid-Forming capability mandatory in JdVVNL tender?"*
2. Show cited answer: *"Yes — Corrigendum 1 changed it from preferred to MANDATORY."*
3. Try compliance check: *"Does 83% RTE meet the MSEDCL requirement?"* → FAIL verdict

## Section 4: Where Does AI Fit? (20 min)

### Group exercise — the AI opportunity map

Draw this on whiteboard:

```
                    HIGH VALUE
                        |
    LOW EFFORT ---------|--------- HIGH EFFORT
                        |
                    LOW VALUE
```

Ask participants to place their daily tasks in the quadrants:
- **High value + Low effort for AI** → Start here
- **High value + High effort for AI** → Plan for Phase 2
- **Low value** → Don't bother

**Facilitator goal:** Identify the 1–2 highest ROI use cases for this specific client.

---

## Section 5: ROI Calculation (25 min)

### Template — fill in together on whiteboard:

**Current cost of the problem:**
- How many hours per week does your team spend on [task]? ___ hours
- What is the hourly cost (salary + overhead)? ₹ ___
- How many incidents per year cost you money? ___ incidents
- What is the average cost per incident? ₹ ___

**Total annual cost = (hours × 52 × hourly rate) + (incidents × cost per incident)**

**AI saves:**
- Hours automation covers: ___% of manual work
- Incidents caught early: ___% of incidents
- Royalties/errors recovered: ₹ ___

**Estimated annual saving = ₹ ___**

**Pilot cost = ₹2–5 lakh**

**ROI = (Annual saving − Pilot cost) / Pilot cost × 100%**

*Walk through this together. The client should calculate their own ROI — much more convincing than you telling them.*

## Section 6: Q&A + Next Steps (20 min)

### Common objections and responses:

**"Our data is messy / incomplete"**
*"That's normal. We start with whatever data you have. Even messy data tells us a lot. We can also generate synthetic data to test concepts before connecting real data."*

**"We tried software before and it didn't work"**
*"The difference here is Human-in-the-Loop design. The AI flags, a human decides. You're always in control. Nothing is fully automated without your approval."*

**"We can't afford it"**
*"Let's look at the ROI calculation we just did together. If the system saves ₹X lakh per year and the pilot costs ₹Y lakh, what's the payback period?"*

**"We need to talk to IT / management first"**
*"Completely understood. Can I prepare a 1-page summary for your IT team and management? And can we schedule a 30-minute call with them next week?"*

### Next steps to propose:
1. Send them the one-pager and this workshop summary
2. Schedule a technical review call with IT (if needed)
3. Define pilot scope and timeline
4. Sign pilot agreement
