## Think of AI Like a Very Smart Employee

A normal employee can read 10 documents per day.
An AI assistant can read 10,000 documents in 10 seconds — and remember everything.

A normal employee can check 50 invoices per week for errors.
An AI system can check 50,000 invoices per night — automatically.

A normal employee notices a machine is making a strange noise only when it breaks.
An AI system detects the warning signs 3 weeks before the breakdown.

**AI does not replace your team. It gives your team superpowers.**

---

## The 3 Things AI Actually Does Well

### 1. It reads and finds information faster than any human
**Example:** You have 200 pages of a government tender document. Instead of reading it yourself for 3 days, you ask: *"Is Grid-Forming capability mandatory?"* — and get the answer in 5 seconds with the exact page reference.

### 2. It spots patterns in data that humans miss
**Example:** One invoice looks normal. But AI notices that this franchise outlet has submitted suspiciously round numbers every Tuesday for 3 months — a pattern no accountant would catch across 500 invoices.

### 3. It predicts problems before they happen
**Example:** Your inverter seems fine today. But AI sees that one component's temperature has been rising 0.3°C per week for 6 weeks. It alerts you: "This unit will fail in 3 weeks." You fix it during scheduled maintenance instead of paying for an emergency call.

## What AI Cannot Do

| AI cannot... | But a human + AI together can... |
|---|---|
| Make final business decisions | Get AI recommendations, then decide |
| Understand context you haven't told it | Learn from your corrections over time |
| Guarantee 100% accuracy | Flag uncertain cases for human review |
| Replace domain expertise | Amplify your experts' productivity 10x |

**This is why all our solutions use Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) design — AI suggests, humans decide.**
